Where Southampton Food has eaten

Every restaurant, café, market and food event we've been to, including when we went and if it was an invite. Updated daily to maintain our ethos of honesty, integrity and openness. Each venue includes a Google Maps link (where a physical location applies) so you can see exactly where it is.

An Invited label means the venue hosted us for that visit. Venues never get a say in what we post. Closed places stay on the list as a record of what the city's had.
